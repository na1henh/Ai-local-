About us

A place to learn AI by making things

AI Club is for anyone who's curious about artificial intelligence, whether you've trained a model before or have never written a line of code. Every meeting has something you can follow along with on your own laptop.

We focus on local AI: open models that run on your own hardware. You'll understand what's actually happening under the hood, you keep your data private, and you never pay for an API key.

What we do

Four ways to get involved

Hands-on workshops

Step-by-step sessions: run your first model, write prompts that work, build a chatbot, fine-tune a small model.

Project teams

Small groups build something real over a semester, then present it at our end-of-term demo day.

Paper & news discussions

We read one paper or story together and talk it through in plain language. No math background needed.

AI ethics & safety

Bias, privacy, security and misinformation. We look at how AI systems fail and how to build them responsibly.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Many members start with zero programming experience. Workshops come with copy-and-paste steps, and there's always someone around to help.

What kind of laptop do I need?

Any laptop from the last few years can run small models. More memory (16 GB or more) lets you run bigger ones, but it isn't required.

Does it cost anything?

No. Membership is free, and because we run models locally, there are no subscriptions or API fees.

Can I join partway through the semester?

Yes. Most meetings stand on their own, so you can jump in at any point.

Ready to start building?

Come to any meeting, or send us an email and we'll add you to the mailing list.
