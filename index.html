The lore

A place to learn AI by cooking

AI Club is for anyone curious about AI, whether you've trained a model before or you think "transformer" is a robot car. Every meeting has something you can follow along with on your own laptop.

We focus on local AI: open models that run on your own hardware. Your data stays on your machine, you understand what's under the hood, and you never pay the API fanum tax.

Our position

Inspired by the most iconic diagram in UN history. Once the hype crosses the red line, the club logs off and touches grass. We're currently at "agents," so it's giving stage two.

What we do

Four ways to lock in

Workshops that go hard

Step-by-step sessions: run your first model, write prompts that actually slap, build a chatbot, fine-tune a small model. Nobody gets left in Ohio.

Project coalitions

Small groups build something real over a semester and demo it at the end. If your coalition collapses, we just hold another election. The Knesset does it constantly and it's fine.

Paper yap sessions

We read one paper together and yap about it in plain language. No math background needed, just vibes and a willingness to be cooked.

AI ethics & safety

Bias, privacy, security and misinformation. How AI systems fail, and how not to build the next skibidi disaster.

FAQ

Questions people yap about

Do I need to know how to code?

No cap, no. Plenty of members start at zero. Workshops are copy-and-paste friendly, and someone will always help you lock in.

What kind of laptop do I need?

Any laptop from the last few years can run small models. 16 GB of memory or more lets you run bigger ones. Laptops from Ohio may require additional prayer.

Does it cost anything?

Free. We run models locally, so there are no subscriptions, no API fees and no fanum tax.

Can I join partway through the semester?

Yes. Most meetings stand on their own. Bibi has made more comebacks than we can count, and so can you.

Is this website serious?

The workshops are real. The aura is real. The red line is metaphorical.

Ready to lock in?

Come to any meeting, or email us and we'll add you to the mailing list. Leaving the mailing list requires a 61-seat majority.
